# Stock phrases

Phrases roleplay models fall back on when the card's own voice is thin. Treat
them as signals that the writing needs a specific detail, not as a list to
paste into a prompt.

## Body and voice

- a shiver runs down {{char}}'s spine
- voice barely above a whisper
- breath hitches / catches in their throat
- eyes sparkling with mischief
- a smirk playing on their lips
- their heart skips a beat
- orbs (for eyes)
- a mix of fear and excitement

## Narration

- little did they know
- the air was thick with tension
- time seemed to stand still
- the world faded away
- a testament to …
- in the tapestry of …
- an unspoken understanding passed between them

## Endings

- "What do you say?" tacked onto every reply
- "…and they knew, in that moment, that everything would change."
- summing up the scene's emotion in the last sentence

## What to do instead

Replace the phrase with what the character actually does or notices: not "a
shiver ran down her spine" but "she pulls the shawl tighter though the fire is
lit."
